---
title: Why I still enjoy mobile development
date: 2026-02-10
tags: [mobile, react native]
excerpt: Mobile keeps me close to the real problems of the people using the app, where performance and experience show up on every tap.
---

Building apps takes intent in every detail. Connections are unreliable, devices differ from one another, and the attention of the person on the other side is short. That setting teaches a lot about simplicity, architecture and experience decisions.

I still like the challenge of making everything feel light despite the constraints. Offline mode, push notifications and local caching are work, but when they land well the result is very satisfying.

In the end, mobile always reminds me that software isn't only code. It's trust, convenience, and making someone's day a little easier.
